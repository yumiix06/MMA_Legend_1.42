# MMA Legend 1.28.0 — lead-developer release audit

## Decision

**Approved as the new stable baseline**, replacing 1.27.0. Save schema is JSON
v14. Keep the clean 1.27.0 ZIP as the rollback point.

The release was not accepted after feature tests alone. Deterministic long-world
simulation exposed two separate population failures; both were corrected and
the complete release gates were rerun.

## Implemented scope

- Complete Regional, twice-yearly National, twice-yearly European and yearly
  World paths for MMA, BJJ / No-Gi, Boxing, Kickboxing, Wrestling, Judo and
  Combat Sambo.
- Sport-specific national teams and medal tables.
- Four-year Olympic path for Boxing, Wrestling and Judo.
- Larger seven-sport NPC population, explicit lifelong side-sport specialists,
  recorded crossover prospects and nationality-linked generated names.
- Persistent NPC form, signed streak, fight history and matchmaking memory.
- Promotion/division roster floors, vacancy-led same-tier movement and earned
  UFC recruitment.
- Authoritative organization belts with reign start, defense count and title
  lineage.
- Save-v14 persistence for all new career and world state.

## Defects found and fixed

1. **NPC results did not update form.** Scheduled fights changed W-L-D but not
   `recent_results`, streak or usable fight history. Rankings and recruitment
   therefore evaluated stale careers.
2. **The old promotion ladder collapsed over time.** A pre-fix 20-year world
   ended with PFL empty, UFC at 18 fighters and p90 inactivity at 616 weeks.
   The first reconstruction still fell from 560 to 373 active pros and left
   UFC at 11, so it was rejected and reworked.
3. **Random opening rosters could create two-fighter divisions.** Same-tier
   transfers were blocked by an incorrect `>=` comparison; one observed pair
   met 15 times in five years. Opening roster balancing, lateral vacancy moves,
   newcomer placement and persistent rematch costs reduced the final 20-year
   maximum to eight.
4. **UFC recruitment could starve despite healthy feeder promotions.** Regional
   champions were barred from moving and the résumé threshold was too narrow
   for the simulated result distribution. Proven champions may now vacate a
   feeder belt to sign with UFC, while feeder floors remain protected.
5. **Amateur intake could freeze at 0-0.** New prospects did not build an MMA
   résumé and therefore never became eligible to turn professional.
6. **The side-sport mix could starve MMA late.** Permanent specialists remain a
   major population, but intake/crossover proportions and MMA activity now
   sustain a professional pipeline across a 20-year career.
7. **Champions and rankings could diverge.** Boards could treat a different
   score leader as champion or show the belt holder at numbered #1. Belts are
   now authoritative and title defenses no longer reset reign state.
8. **Cancelled bouts retained booking locks.** Open-fighter collection now
   ignores cancelled card rows.
9. **Legacy sport selection could reset to MMA.** Save upgrade now respects the
   old `compete_in` value when the v13 active-sport field is absent.
10. **Tournament entry energy was insufficient for the final.** Three-match
    championships and four-match Olympics now require enough starting energy
    to finish the bracket.
11. **Long careers exhausted small regional name combinations.** Generated
    fighters now retain nationality-linked names with expanded middle-initial
    combinations instead of degrading to numbered placeholders.

## Deterministic 20-year audit

Seed 12801, 840 amateur + 560 professional starting world, 1,040 simulated
weeks:

- 2,769 total historical fighters; 837 active amateurs and 596 active pros.
- All seven amateur sports populated; 342 active lifelong side-sport athletes.
- All 12 professional organizations active; UFC retained 78 fighters.
- 15-week median inactivity, 36-week p90, 49-week p99, 71-week maximum.
- 5,726 unique professional pairings; maximum eight meetings for one pair
  across the full twenty years.
- 2,044 title bouts across all 96 organization/weight-class combinations;
  median title interval 40 weeks and p90 80 weeks.
- 21 Olympic medalists generated by the three eligible sports.
- Zero duplicate-booking weeks, invalid title slots or UFC champion/belt
  mismatches.
- 1,085 original NPCs changed recent form and 1,064 changed streak state,
  demonstrating that scheduled results feed career logic.

## Release gates

- `python3 -m compileall -q mma_legend`
- `python3 -m mma_legend.tests` — all discovered historical and current modules,
  including 2,400-fight engine fairness.
- `python3 -m mma_legend.tests_128` — all v1.28 sport/world checks.
- `python3 -m mma_legend.diagnostics` — v1.28.0, 58 countries, 194 authored
  opponents, 42 combat moments, 511 events; all systems OK.
- Clean-package verification repeats compilation, v1.28 tests and diagnostics
  from a fresh extraction before release.

## Packaging boundary

Player saves, autosaves, playtest traces, debug logs, bytecode caches and prior
ZIPs are excluded. The user-provided saves remain diagnostic references only;
the release makes no promise that they are a permanent compatibility target.
